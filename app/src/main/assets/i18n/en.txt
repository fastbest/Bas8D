Got it
• The list button at the top right shows / hides the queue.
• Use the magnifier to search in the queue.
• Pressing Previous near the start goes to the previous track; after 4 seconds it goes to the start of the track.
• Repeat button: off → all → one.
• The shuffle button plays in random order.
• Adjust bass, treble, equalizer, 8D audio and volume boost from the Equalizer menu.
• A-B repeat: select once (A), then again (B); the part in between repeats. A third selection turns it off.
• Tap a track: it plays.
• Three dots on a track: remove from list, move up / down, play next, add to another playlist.
• Use "Save playlist" to name and keep the current queue; you'll find it in the "Playlists" tab.
• On the home screen, use "Add to queue" from a track's three dots to add tracks.
• Single tap: shows / hides the controls.
• Double tap: right side seeks forward, left side back (time can be changed in Control settings).
• Swipe up-down on the left: brightness. On the right: volume.
• Swipe horizontally: seek forward / back in the video.
• Lock: blocks touches; to unlock, tap the screen and hold the lock button for 3 seconds.
• Play as audio: turns off the picture and plays only the sound.
• Pop-up player: the video plays in a small window over other apps.
